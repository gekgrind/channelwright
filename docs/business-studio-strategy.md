# Three-fundamentals strategy

Every accepted channel concept now pauses on a versioned business strategy before video production becomes available. This applies to user-defined, discovered, and reference-channel onboarding.

The strategy contract connects:

`YouTube content → Pillar Video → free-resource opt-in → resource delivery → paid-product offer → additional monetization`

Each version contains an original brand-token system, a complete Pillar Video plan, multiple free-resource options, multiple paid-product options, and a preliminary monetization overview that separates fact basis from assumptions. Independent QA checks choice, connectedness, and originality. Fixture strategies label pricing, demand, and monetization as unverified hypotheses.

Requesting changes creates a new strategy version. Approval binds the exact current version. After approval, free-resource and paid-product selections are recorded separately so the approved strategy is not rewritten. A selected Pillar Video is created as `kind: PILLAR` with its source business-strategy version, then uses the existing video strategy, research, script, QA, revision, approval, and optional platform-adaptation flow.

No selection deploys a page, captures public email, charges a customer, renders media, or publishes content. Those capabilities require their own builder, QA, provider, and deployment gates.
